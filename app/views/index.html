Make your service part of the NHS App

When more services connect to the NHS App, more people can find and use them. Use free, self-serve components to integrate your service with the NHS App.

These tools are free to anyone who works for:

  • NHS Trusts
  • Integrated Care Boards
  • GP surgeries
  • NHS pharmacies
  • NHS dentists
  • providers of NHS-commissioned services
