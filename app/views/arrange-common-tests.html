Arrange common tests

People choose how to take a common test. The result goes back to their clinician and to the patient.

People arrange a test in the NHS App – a blood test, blood pressure check, cholesterol or HIV test – and choose how, when and where to take it.

Use it for a one-off test, or build it into a service.

How it works

1. Choose a test

Offered by a clinician, or chosen by the patient.

2. Pick how to take it

Whichever route suits them.

3. Take the test

In person, or a kit arrives at home.

4. Get the result

Once a clinician releases it, it comes back, explained.

Patients choose how to get tested

You decide which routes to offer. Patients choose what suits them.

How results come back

However the test is taken, the result comes back the same way:

  • it reaches the responsible clinician who owns the test
  • the patient sees it in the NHS App, explained in plain language with clear next steps
